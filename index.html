IN THE TESLA

  • Open the page from its https:// address and bookmark it. Location never works from a plain http address or inside another app's preview.
  • When the car asks to share your location, tap Allow.
  • If you tapped Block: tap the location icon in the address bar and allow it. If the question never comes back, clearing the browser data in the car's Service menu resets site permissions.
  • Receiver speed means the GPS reports speed directly, which is best. From position means speed is worked out from movement, which lags a little more.
  • Readings usually come about once a second. If they stop for 3 seconds, the engine eases to idle until they return.
